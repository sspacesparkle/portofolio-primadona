Brand & Identity
Sidecar

Idea
Sidecar was a coffee and eatery concept originally located alongside an automotive showroom, creating an unconventional meeting point between coffee culture and car lifestyle. The brand offered an easygoing space for coffee, light bites, and casual conversations within a setting that naturally carried an automotive character.

Insight
The unique environment gave Sidecar a personality that went beyond a conventional coffee shop. Content had the opportunity to play with the relationship between coffee, movement, automotive culture, and everyday hangouts, making the space feel distinct without needing overly complex communication.
