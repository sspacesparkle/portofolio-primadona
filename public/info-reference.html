Hi, I’m Nayla, a Social Media Specialist and Creative Strategist with 3+ years of experience, particularly within the F&B and consumer space.

I enjoy turning unexpected, sometimes slightly wild ideas into content that feels relevant, intentional, and true to the brand behind it. For me, social media is not simply about chasing engagement or following every trend. A strong piece of content should have its own voice, visual language, purpose, and personality, something distinctive enough that people can recognize the brand before seeing its name.

My experience covers the full creative process, from audience and trend research, strategy, content planning, creative direction, copywriting, and production to execution and performance analysis. Along the way, I’ve developed content that reached millions of views, built food-editorial concepts, and begun exploring character-driven storytelling through a social media mini-series.

I’m curious, outspoken, observant, adaptable, and always interested in trying something new. I may not always choose the fastest route, but I care about finding the right one. This portfolio is a living archive of that process: the ideas I’ve explored, the work I’ve built, and the possibilities I’m still excited to pursue.
