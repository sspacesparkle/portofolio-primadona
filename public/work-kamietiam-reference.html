Kamietiam

Idea
KAMIETIAM is a modern-classic kopitiam concept inspired by familiar flavors, traditional coffee culture, and the warmth of home. Built around the spirit of everyday meals and conversations, the brand brings classic kopitiam comfort into a more polished and contemporary dining experience.

Insight
The brand needed to feel traditional without becoming old-fashioned, and modern without losing its soul. Its social presence was developed to communicate warmth, familiarity, and comfort through simple language, nostalgic food moments, and a tone that feels close to home while still refined.
