Coarse & Fine 
Idea
Coarse & Fine is a neighborhood specialty coffee shop under Satria Group, built around thoughtfully crafted coffee and beans roasted in-house. For nearly a decade, it has become a familiar part of its communities in Alam Sutera and Ubud, Bali—creating spaces where good coffee naturally becomes part of everyday routines.
Insight
After ten years, the challenge was not to reinvent Coarse & Fine, but to keep the brand relevant without losing the familiarity people had grown to love. Its social presence needed to reflect the same balance found in the coffee shop: mature yet approachable, knowledgeable without feeling intimidating, and distinctive while remaining part of the neighborhood.

IoLo
Idea
IOLO Pizza is a casual pizza concept within the Coarse & Fine ecosystem, created to bring a more social, shareable food experience into the coffee shop environment.
